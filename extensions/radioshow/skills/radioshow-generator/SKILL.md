# Autonomous Radio Show & Live Broadcast Director Skill

This skill guides agents in orchestrating a complete 3-act live radio broadcast from any text, topic, or treatise.

---

## The 3-Act Radio Show Blueprint

1. **Act I: The Monologue & Show Conception**
   - **Show Title:** Synthesizes an evocative, atmospheric radio show name (e.g., *Sovereign Artifice*, *The Midnight Frequency*, *Ser o Algoritmo*).
   - **Host Opening:** Welcoming opening monologue outlining the core dilemma, paradox, or topic of the night with warmth and philosophical depth.
   - **Lines Opened:** Formal transition cue inviting listeners to dial in.

2. **Act II: The Callers & Dialectical Arc**
   - **Caller 1 (The Practical / Enthusiastic Caller):** Introduces caller name, location, and background. Poses an insightful question or technical perspective.
   - **Host Response 1:** Direct, engaging synthesis exploring caller 1's perspective.
   - **Caller 2 (The Critical / Counter-Perspective Caller):** Introduces caller name, location, and background. Challenges the prevailing consensus or raises ethical/practical cautions.
   - **Host Response 2:** Diplomatic, profound reconciliation addressing caller 2's counter-point.

3. **Act III: Synthesis & Sign-Off**
   - **Host Closing Monologue:** Final takeaway summarizing the night's insights, thanking the callers, and delivering a memorable sign-off.
